PASE: Leveraging the Phonological Prior of WavLM
for Low-Hallucination Generative Speech Enhancement

Xiaobin Rong1,2, Qinwe Hu1,2, Mansur Yesilbursa2, Kamil Wojcicki2, Jing Lu1
1Key Laboratory of Modern Acoustics, Nanjing University
2Collaboration AI, Cisco Systems, Inc.
📁 Code Repository 📄 ArXiv Paper

Abstract

We propose PASE, a novel generative speech enhancement framework that leverages the phonological prior of WavLM to achieve low-hallucination performance. Traditional generative speech enhancement methods often suffer from hallucination issues, where the model generates non-existent audio content. Our approach addresses this challenge by incorporating phonological constraints from pre-trained WavLM models.

PASE effectively suppresses hallucination artifacts while maintaining high speech quality and intelligibility. Experimental results demonstrate that our method outperforms state-of-the-art approaches across multiple metrics, particularly in hallucination reduction and speech naturalness preservation.

Keywords: Speech Enhancement, Generative Models, Hallucination Reduction, Phonological Prior, WavLM
